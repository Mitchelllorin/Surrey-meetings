# Surrey Meetings

Find AA, NA, CA, CMA and SA meetings in Surrey and across the Lower Mainland. Open it on a phone, tap **Add to Home screen** and it opens like an app.

**Live:** https://mitchelllorin.github.io/Surrey-meetings/

- Day-by-day list with the next meeting up top
- Filter by how far from Surrey, by fellowship, and in person or online
- Directions and Zoom links on every meeting
- A private phone list for the numbers you get at meetings, saved only on your own phone
- Help lines: 911, 988, BC mental health and drug-referral lines, AA, NA, CA

Meeting times come from the Easy Does It Club, AA Greater Vancouver Intergroup, AA BC/Yukon Area 79, NA BC Region, Cocaine Anonymous BC, Crystal Meth Anonymous and SA Metro Vancouver. Times change, so call ahead if it matters.

**Free Meals:** https://mitchelllorin.github.io/Surrey-meetings/meals/ — free and cheap meals across the Lower Mainland by day, with what's serving next. Add it to your home screen the same way.

Built by Mitchell McKnight in Surrey.
